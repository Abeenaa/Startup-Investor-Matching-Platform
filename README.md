Innobiz Startup Ecosystem Platform
Overview

The Innobiz Startup Ecosystem Platform is a digital platform designed to support the Ethiopian startup ecosystem by providing a centralized system for discovering, tracking, and connecting startups with investors and ecosystem stakeholders.

The platform aims to improve visibility of startups, enable structured tracking of their growth stages, and facilitate investment opportunities within Ethiopia's innovation ecosystem.

This project is being developed as part of an internship program under the Ministry of Innovation and Technology (MInT).

Problem Statement

Currently, information about startups in Ethiopia is fragmented and difficult to access. Investors, ecosystem builders, and government institutions often lack a centralized platform where they can:

discover promising startups

track startup progress and stages

connect startups with potential investors

analyze ecosystem growth

This platform aims to solve these challenges by creating a centralized startup discovery and investment matching system.

Objectives

The main objectives of this platform are:

Create a centralized startup listing platform

Track startup growth stages and development

Provide investment matching opportunities

Improve visibility of startups across Ethiopia

Support the national innovation ecosystem

Core Features (MVP)

Startups can be listed with detailed information including:

Sector / Industry

Description

Each startup will have a detailed profile page showing:

company overview

founders

sector

progress stage

funding information

Investment Matching

The platform will help connect startups with potential investors by allowing:

investors to explore startups

Search and Filtering

Users will be able to search and filter startups based on:

sector

location

startup stage

funding stage
